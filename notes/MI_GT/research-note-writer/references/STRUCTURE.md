# Learning Module Structure Guide

This structure ensures that every research note builds intuition and maintains rigor.

## 1. Urgensi Eksplorasi & Fondasi Teoretis
- **Context:** Why is this topic important?
- **Axioms:** What are the basic assumptions?
- **Physics Analogy:** Connect to a physical system (e.g., potential wells, spin systems).

## 2. Reduksionisme (Kasus Minimal)
- **Simplify:** Break down into the simplest non-trivial case (e.g., 2-qubit or 2-asset).
- **Algebra:** Explicit basic algebra before generalizing to $N$ variables.

## 3. Derivasi "Scratchpad" & Formalisme Matematika
- **Step-by-Step:** No gaps in logic.
- **Equation Index:** Use (1), (2), etc.
- **Visualisation Block:** Use `>` for matrix/vector flow.

## 4. Jembatan Logika (Economic vs. Physical Insight)
- **Economic to Physical:** Explain *why* an economic concept (e.g., Nash Equilibrium) is equivalent to a physical one (e.g., Ground State).
- **Physical Interpretation:** Use physical terms like *energy landscapes* or *thermal fluctuations*.

## 5. Analogi & Verifikasi Parameter
- **Parameter Analysis:** How does $\gamma$ or $\sigma$ affect the system?
- **Raw Visualization:** Describe the expected graph or phase diagram.
