\section{Perkalian Matriks 2-aset Markowitz}
Untuk sistem 2 aset, fungsi energi Markowitz adalah:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L} = \begin{pmatrix} x_1 & x_2 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} \sigma_1^2 & \sigma_{12} \\ \sigma_{21} & \sigma_2^2 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \end{pmatrix} - \lambda \begin{pmatrix} \mu_1 & \mu_2 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \end{pmatrix}
\end{equation}
Langkah 1 (Baris $\times$ Matriks):
\begin{equation}
\begin{pmatrix} x_1 \sigma_1^2 + x_2 \sigma_{21} & x_1 \sigma_{12} + x_2 \sigma_2^2 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \end{pmatrix}
\end{equation}
Langkah 2 (Skalar):
\begin{equation}
(x_1^2 \sigma_1^2 + x_1 x_2 \sigma_{21}) + (x_1 x_2 \sigma_{12} + x_2^2 \sigma_2^2) = \sigma_1^2 x_1^2 + \sigma_2^2 x_2^2 + (\sigma_{12} + \sigma_{21}) x_1 x_2
\end{equation}
Karena matriks kovarians simetris ($\sigma_{12} = \sigma_{21}$), diperoleh:
\begin{equation}
\sigma_1^2 x_1^2 + \sigma_2^2 x_2^2 + 2\sigma_{12} x_1 x_2
\end{equation}
Ditambah suku linear imbal hasil:
\begin{equation}
-\lambda(\mu_1 x_1 + \mu_2 x_2)
\end{equation}
Sehingga bentuk akhirnya adalah:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L} = \sigma_1^2 x_1^2 + \sigma_2^2 x_2^2 + 2\sigma_{12}x_1 x_2 - \lambda(\mu_1 x_1 + \mu_2 x_2)
\end{equation}
